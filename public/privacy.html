Privacy Policy

Last updated:

What we collect

When you book a session we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and (optionally) phone number
  • The artist, date, and time of your booking
  • The payment method you selected and the amount of your deposit
  • Any reference images you choose to share with your artist

We do not collect or store your bank account details, card numbers, or PayPal credentials. Payment is made directly through your chosen method.

Why we collect it

  • To reserve your session with the artist you chose
  • To send you a receipt and booking confirmation by email
  • To contact you if there is a problem with your booking
  • To keep records of payments for accounting and dispute resolution

Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary information with:

  • Brevo — our email delivery provider, which sends your booking confirmation and receipt. Brevo receives your name, email address, and receipt contents.
  • Our hosting provider — which stores the booking database on servers we control.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Booking and payment records are kept for 7 years to satisfy Australian tax and accounting requirements. Reference images are deleted 12 months after your session unless you ask us to keep them. You can request deletion of your personal information at any time, subject to our legal record-keeping obligations.

How we protect it

Your data is stored in a database accessible only to studio staff. The admin panel is protected by an access token stored in a server-side environment variable, not in the website code. Email delivery uses an encrypted API connection. We do not store payment credentials on our servers.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information we no longer need to keep
  • Complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if you believe we have mishandled your data

Cookies

This site uses a single session cookie to keep you signed in to the admin panel if you are a staff member. It does not track you across other websites. There are no advertising or analytics cookies on this site.
